Featured Project: KrishiSetu

An agricultural innovation addressing middleman exploitation in rural supply chains.

Agritech Direct Marketplace

KrishiSetu | कृषि सेतु

Founded & Conceptualized by Paritosh Tiwari

The Problem in Agriculture

In traditional agricultural mandis, smallholder farmers often lose a major fraction of their harvest earnings to commission agents, brokers, and multi-layered intermediaries. Farmers are burdened with delayed payouts, unfair weighing charges, and distress sales due to lack of direct access to real buyers.

The KrishiSetu Solution

KrishiSetu provides a direct platform allowing farmers to sell their agricultural produce directly to consumers, restaurants, and bulk buyers. By eliminating the middleman layer, the platform guarantees that farmers retain fair market prices while buyers receive verified, transparent produce.

Core Pillars of the Platform:

1. Zero Intermediaries
Direct buyer-to-farmer trade relationship with full transparency.
2. Fair Market Benchmarking
Pricing linked to real mandi rates to prevent distress under-selling.
3. Rural Accessibility
Simplified listing interface designed specifically for smallholder ease of use.
